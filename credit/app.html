Quick help

Can I apply for a loan now?

No. MAAKIT Credit is an informational prototype; no lender partner is integrated and no applications are accepted.

Is MAAKIT an RBI-registered lender?

MAAKIT does not claim to be an RBI-registered lender. Before any actual offer is presented, verify the regulated lending partner.

Does this demo need my PAN?

No. Never share PAN or bank details to try this prototype.

How can I estimate EMI?

Use the calculator and change amount, example annual rate, months and fee. It is not an actual APR or offer.

What is KFS?

The Key Facts Statement describes important loan terms, APR and relevant charges provided by a regulated lender.

MONEY KNOW-HOW

The smarter borrowing guide

Understand the language behind every loan decision — simple and practical.

EMI

Equated Monthly Instalment. Your scheduled monthly repayment, typically covering principal plus interest.

APR

Annual Percentage Rate represents the all-inclusive annualised borrowing cost and is not the same as the nominal interest rate displayed in our calculator.

KFS

Key Facts Statement. Your actual regulated lender must provide key loan terms and charges.

Cooling-off period

Applicable digital loans have a defined exit option during the cooling-off period, subject to RBI rules and the lender's terms.

Processing fee

A potential charge. Compare its impact alongside interest, taxes, total outflow and the disclosed APR.

Late payment

Check any disclosed penal charges and the consequences of missed payments before accepting an actual offer.
