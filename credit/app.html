Quick help

Can I apply for a loan now?

No. MAAKIT Credit is an informational prototype; no lender partner is integrated and no applications are accepted.

Is MAAKIT an RBI-registered lender?

MAAKIT does not claim to be an RBI-registered lender. Before any actual offer is presented, verify the regulated lending partner.

Does this demo need my PAN?

No. Never share PAN or bank details to try this prototype.

How can I estimate EMI?

Use the calculator and change amount, example annual rate, months and fee. It is not an actual APR or offer.

What is KFS?

The Key Facts Statement describes important loan terms, APR and relevant charges provided by a regulated lender.
