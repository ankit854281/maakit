How it works

From the shop, to your door.

  1. Step 1

    Order

    Tell us the shop and items — in the app, or by phone and WhatsApp until the app is out.

  2. Step 2

    Shop accepts and packs

    The shop confirms the items and its own price, then packs your order.

  3. Step 3

    Sarathi picks up

    A nearby Sarathi collects it, confirmed with a pickup code.

  4. Step 4

    Delivered to your door

    Follow the order and share the delivery code when it arrives.

Why Maakit

Small towns have shops, vehicles and skilled people, but no single app that connects them. Maakit is building that platform, starting in eastern Uttar Pradesh.

01

An underserved market

Large delivery apps focus on big cities. Towns and villages still run on phone calls and word of mouth.

02

Many services, one platform

Shopping, rides, parcels, vehicle and service bookings, coaching and school software share one base.

03

Technology built in-house

Customer, shop, rider and admin software are built and run by Maakit itself.

04

Honest money model

Customers pay the shop directly in cash or UPI. Maakit earns from delivery fees and services, never by holding customer money.

Questions

Good to know

Can I order on this website?+

No. Orders will be placed in the Maakit app. Until the app is on Google Play, order by phone or WhatsApp on 84293 93903.

Are prices higher than in the shop?+

No. The price of the goods belongs to the shop. Maakit charges only a delivery fee.

How do I pay?+

Cash on delivery, or UPI straight to the shop’s own UPI ID.

How can my shop join?+

Choose “Join as a shop”, fill in your details, and our team will call you.

How do I become a Sarathi?+

Open the Sarathi Partner app, add your Aadhaar and PAN, and choose your vehicle and area.
