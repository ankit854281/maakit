Unit I — Kinematics & Equations of Motion

Liquid ko kaise describe karein, continuity, Euler, Bernoulli.

1.1 Lagrangian aur Eulerian method

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — difference (short question)
💡 Seedhi baat Nadi ko do tareeke se dekh sakte hain:
  • Lagrangian: ek patta (particle) pakdo aur uske saath-saath chalo — woh kahan-kahan gaya. Position \(x = x(a,b,c,t)\), jahan (a, b, c) = shuru ki position.
  • Eulerian: pul (bridge) par khade raho aur dekho ki us jagah se guzarne wala paani kitni speed se ja raha hai. Velocity \(\mathbf q=\mathbf q(x,y,z,t)\).
LagrangianEulerian
Particle ko follow karoFixed point par dekho
Variables: a, b, c, tVariables: x, y, z, t
Har particle ki history milti haiField (poora picture) milta hai — zyada use hota hai
📝 Material (total) derivative Particle ke saath chalte hue badlav: $$\frac{D}{Dt}=\frac{\partial}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+v\frac{\partial}{\partial y}+w\frac{\partial}{\partial z}=\frac{\partial}{\partial t}+(\mathbf q\cdot\nabla)$$ Acceleration \(=\dfrac{D\mathbf q}{Dt}\).
🧠 TrickLagrange = "Le chalo" (particle ke saath). Euler = "Ek jagah" khade raho.

1.2 Equation of continuity

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — derivation 10 marks
💡 Seedhi baat Paani na banta hai, na mitta hai (mass conservation). Ek chhote dabbe me jitna paani ghusa minus jitna nikla = andar ka paani kitna badha.
📝 Eulerian form + derivation $$\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf q)=0$$
  1. Ek fixed volume V lo, surface S. Andar ka mass \(=\int_V\rho\,dV\).
  2. Mass badhne ki rate \(=\int_V\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}dV\).
  3. Surface se bahar jaata mass \(=\int_S\rho\,\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\int_V\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf q)\,dV\) (Gauss divergence theorem).
  4. Badhna = −(bahar jaana): \(\int_V\left[\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf q)\right]dV=0\).
  5. V koi bhi ho sakta hai ⇒ bracket = 0. ∎
📝 Doosre roop
  • \(\dfrac{D\rho}{Dt}+\rho\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf q=0\)
  • Incompressible (ρ constant): \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf q=0\), yaani \(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial w}{\partial z}=0\)
  • Irrotational + incompressible: \(\nabla^2\phi=0\) (Laplace equation)
  • Lagrangian form: \(\rho J=\rho_0\), jahan \(J=\dfrac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(a,b,c)}\)
✍️ ExampleKya \(u=-\frac{2xyz}{(x^2+y^2)^2},\ v=\frac{(x^2-y^2)z}{(x^2+y^2)^2},\ w=\frac{y}{x^2+y^2}\) ek possible incompressible motion hai? Method: \(u_x+v_y+w_z\) nikalo — 0 aaye to haan. (Yahan 0 aata hai ⇒ possible.)
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"Derive the equation of continuity in Eulerian / Lagrangian form." / "Show that … is a possible motion of incompressible fluid."

1.3 Boundary surface

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baatBoundary (jaise bartan ki deewar ya chalti hui surface) ke paar paani nahi jaata. Jo particle surface par hai, woh surface par hi rehta hai.
📝 ConditionSurface \(F(x,y,z,t)=0\) boundary surface hai iff $$\frac{DF}{Dt}=\frac{\partial F}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}+v\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}+w\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}=0.$$ Matlab: surface ki normal velocity = liquid ki normal velocity.
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"Show that \(\frac{x^2}{a^2k^2t^4}+kt^2\left(\frac{y^2}{b^2}+\frac{z^2}{c^2}\right)=1\) is a possible form of boundary surface." — method: F banao, DF/Dt = 0 se u, v, w check karo.

1.4 Streamlines, pathlines, streak lines

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — definitions + numerical
💡 Seedhi baat
  • Streamline: ek pal ki photo — har point par velocity ki disha jodne wali line (tangent = velocity).
  • Pathline: ek particle ka poora raasta (video jaise).
  • Streak line: ek fixed point se guzre saare particles ko jodne wali line (jaise agarbatti ka dhuan).
Steady flow me teeno ek hi hote hain.
📝 Equations Streamlines: \(\dfrac{dx}{u}=\dfrac{dy}{v}=\dfrac{dz}{w}\) (t ko constant maano)
Pathlines: \(\dfrac{dx}{dt}=u,\ \dfrac{dy}{dt}=v,\ \dfrac{dz}{dt}=w\)
✍️ Example \(u=x,\ v=-y\) (2-D). Streamlines?
  1. \(\frac{dx}{x}=\frac{dy}{-y}\)
  2. Integrate: \(\log x=-\log y+\log c\)
  3. \(\boxed{xy=c}\) — rectangular hyperbolas.

1.5 Velocity potential, vorticity, rotational / irrotational

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
💡 Seedhi baat Paani me ek chhoti si "chakri" (paddle wheel) daalo. Ghoome ⇒ rotational. Na ghoome ⇒ irrotational.
Ghoomne ka naap = vorticity \(\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf q\).
Irrotational flow me ek function \(\phi\) milta hai jisse velocity aati hai: \(\mathbf q=-\nabla\phi\) — yahi velocity potential.
📝 Definitions
  • Vorticity \(\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf q=(\xi,\eta,\zeta)\)
  • Irrotational ⇔ \(\boldsymbol\omega=\mathbf 0\) ⇔ velocity potential exists, \(u=-\phi_x,\ v=-\phi_y,\ w=-\phi_z\)
  • Vortex line: jiska tangent har point par ω ki disha me: \(\frac{dx}{\xi}=\frac{dy}{\eta}=\frac{dz}{\zeta}\)
  • Vortex tube: closed curve ke har point se guzarti vortex lines se bana tube.
✍️ Example\(\phi=x^2-y^2\) ⇒ \(u=-2x,\ v=2y\). Check: \(\nabla^2\phi=2-2=0\) ✓ (incompressible). Streamlines \(\frac{dx}{-2x}=\frac{dy}{2y}\) ⇒ \(xy=c\).
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"Show that the motion is irrotational and find the velocity potential." / "Find vortex lines for \(u=y+z,\ v=z+x,\ w=x+y\)" (yahan curl = 0, to irrotational).

1.6 Euler's equation of motion

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — derivation 10 marks
💡 Seedhi baatNewton ka F = ma, paani ke liye. Mass × acceleration = body force (gravity) + pressure ka dhakka.
📝 Equation + derivation $$\frac{D\mathbf q}{Dt}=\mathbf F-\frac1\rho\nabla p$$
  1. Ek moving volume V lo (surface S). Mass \(\int\rho\,dV\) constant.
  2. Body force \(=\int_V\rho\mathbf F\,dV\). Pressure force \(=-\int_S p\,\mathbf n\,dS=-\int_V\nabla p\,dV\) (Gauss).
  3. Newton: \(\int_V\rho\frac{D\mathbf q}{Dt}dV=\int_V(\rho\mathbf F-\nabla p)\,dV\).
  4. V koi bhi ⇒ \(\rho\frac{D\mathbf q}{Dt}=\rho\mathbf F-\nabla p\). ∎
Components: \(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+v\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+w\frac{\partial u}{\partial z}=X-\frac1\rho\frac{\partial p}{\partial x}\) (v, w ke liye bhi aise hi).
📝 Vector form (Lamb) \(\dfrac{\partial\mathbf q}{\partial t}-\mathbf q\times\boldsymbol\omega=-\nabla\Big(\tfrac12q^2+\Omega+\int\tfrac{dp}{\rho}\Big)\) jab \(\mathbf F=-\nabla\Omega\). Bernoulli isi se nikalta hai.
📝 Lagrange's equations (Lagrangian form) $$\Big(\ddot x-X\Big)\frac{\partial x}{\partial a}+\Big(\ddot y-Y\Big)\frac{\partial y}{\partial a}+\Big(\ddot z-Z\Big)\frac{\partial z}{\partial a}=-\frac1\rho\frac{\partial p}{\partial a}$$ (b aur c ke liye bhi same.) Euler ki equation ko \(\partial x/\partial a\) etc. se multiply karke jodne par milti hai.

1.7 Bernoulli's equation

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — har paper me
💡 Seedhi baatJahan paani tez behta hai wahan pressure kam. Pipe patla ⇒ speed zyada ⇒ pressure kam. Energy ka hisaab: pressure energy + kinetic energy + height energy = constant.
📝 Do roop (a) Irrotational, unsteady (F = −∇Ω, barotropic): $$\int\frac{dp}{\rho}+\frac12q^2+\Omega-\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial t}=C(t)$$ (b) Steady flow — streamline ke along: $$\frac p\rho+\frac12q^2+gz=\text{constant}$$ Derivation idea: Lamb form me irrotational (ω = 0) aur \(\mathbf q=-\nabla\phi\) rakho ⇒ \(\nabla\big(-\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial t}+\frac12q^2+\Omega+\int\frac{dp}\rho\big)=0\) ⇒ bracket sirf t ka function. Steady me: Lamb form ko q se dot karo, \(\mathbf q\cdot(\mathbf q\times\boldsymbol\omega)=0\), to streamline par constant.
✍️ ExampleTanki me h gehraai par chhed: \(\frac{p_0}{\rho}+0+gh=\frac{p_0}{\rho}+\frac12v^2+0\) ⇒ \(\boxed{v=\sqrt{2gh}}\) (Torricelli).
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"State and prove Bernoulli's theorem for steady/unsteady irrotational motion."

1.8 Impulsive action

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baatAchanak dhakka (jaise bartan ko jhatke se chalana). Bahut kam time me bahut bada pressure — use impulsive pressure \(\varpi\) kehte hain. Velocity achanak \(\mathbf q_1\) se \(\mathbf q_2\) ho jaati hai.
📝 Equation $$\mathbf q_2-\mathbf q_1=\mathbf I-\frac1\rho\nabla\varpi$$ (\(\mathbf I\) = impulsive body force per unit mass.) Liquid rest se shuru ho aur I = 0 ho ⇒ motion irrotational, aur \(\varpi=\rho\phi+C\). Incompressible me \(\nabla^2\varpi=0\).

Unit II — 2-D Flow: Sources, Sinks, Images, Circulation

Complex potential se poora flow ek line me likhna.

2.1 Stream function aur complex potential

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — base of Unit II, III
💡 Seedhi baat2-D incompressible flow me ek function \(\psi\) hota hai — \(\psi\) = constant lines hi streamlines hain. Do streamlines ke beech ka paani ka flow = \(\psi_2-\psi_1\).
Irrotational bhi ho to \(\phi\) aur \(\psi\) ko jod kar ek complex function bana lo: \(w=\phi+i\psi\) — sab kuch isi se.
📝 Relations (q = −∇φ) $$u=-\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x}=-\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial y},\qquad v=-\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}$$ Yeh Cauchy–Riemann equations hain ⇒ \(w=\phi+i\psi\) analytic function of \(z=x+iy\). $$\frac{dw}{dz}=-u+iv,\qquad q=\left|\frac{dw}{dz}\right|$$ \(\phi\) aur \(\psi\) dono Laplace satisfy karte hain; \(\phi\)= const aur \(\psi\) = const lines ek doosre ke perpendicular.
FlowComplex potential w
Uniform stream U (x-disha me)\(-Uz\)
Source strength m at \(z_0\)\(-m\log(z-z_0)\)
Sink strength m at \(z_0\)\(+m\log(z-z_0)\)
Doublet strength μ at \(z_0\)\(\dfrac{\mu}{z-z_0}\)
Vortex (circulation Γ) at \(z_0\)\(\dfrac{i\Gamma}{2\pi}\log(z-z_0)\)
✍️ Example\(w=z^2\) ⇒ \(\phi=x^2-y^2,\ \psi=2xy\). Streamlines \(xy\)= const. \(dw/dz=2z\) ⇒ \(u=-2x,\ v=2y\), speed \(q=2r\).

2.2 Sources, sinks aur doublets

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
💡 Seedhi baat Source: ek point jahan se paani har disha me barabar nikalta hai (jaise nal). Sink: jahan paani andar jaata hai (jaise naali).
Doublet: ek source aur ek sink ko bahut paas laao aur strength badha do (m × distance = μ fixed) — ek naya flow banta hai.
📝 2-D source Strength m: har second \(2\pi m\) paani nikalta hai. Radial velocity \(q_r=\dfrac{m}{r}\), \(\phi=-m\log r,\ \psi=-m\theta\).
Doublet: source m at a, sink m at origin ke paas; \(m\to\infty\), \(a\to0\), \(ma=\mu\): $$w=-m\log(z-a)+m\log z\ \longrightarrow\ \frac{\mu}{z}$$
✍️ Example: source + sinkSource m at (a,0), sink m at (−a,0): \(w=-m\log(z-a)+m\log(z+a)\). Streamlines \(\psi=-m(\theta_1-\theta_2)\)= const ⇒ dono points se guzarte circles.
✍️ 3-D source (Unit IV me kaam aayega)Strength m: \(\phi=\dfrac mr\), \(q_r=\dfrac{m}{r^2}\), har second \(4\pi m\) paani.

2.3 Images — line, circle, sphere

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — circle wala pakka
💡 Seedhi baatDeewar ke paas source ho to paani deewar ke paar nahi ja sakta. Trick: deewar hata do aur shishe (mirror) ki tarah doosri taraf ek nakli source rakh do — uska asar deewar par normal velocity zero kar deta hai. Yahi image hai.

📝 Circle wala proof (short)
  1. Source m at A (OA = f), circle |z| = a. Inverse point A′, OA′ = \(a^2/f\).
  2. System: source m at A, source m at A′, sink m at O: \(w=-m\log(z-f)-m\log(z-\tfrac{a^2}{f})+m\log z\).
  3. Circle par \(z=ae^{i\theta}\) rakho. \(\psi\) = constant aata hai ⇒ circle ek streamline ⇒ uske paar flow nahi. ∎
Short-cut: Milne-Thomson circle theorem (Unit III) se seedha.
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"Find the image of a source with respect to a circle." / "Image of a source in a plane / sphere."

2.4 Theorem of Blasius

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — statement + use
💡 Seedhi baatSteady flow me paani ek fixed cylinder par kitna force lagata hai — ek contour integral se nikal aata hai.
📝 Statement Steady 2-D irrotational flow, fixed cylinder C, complex potential w. Force (X, Y) aur moment M (origin ke around): $$X-iY=\frac{i\rho}{2}\oint_C\left(\frac{dw}{dz}\right)^2dz,\qquad M=\text{Re}\left[-\frac\rho2\oint_C z\left(\frac{dw}{dz}\right)^2dz\right]$$ Proof idea: Surface ke chhote hisse par pressure force \(p\,ds\); Bernoulli se \(p=C-\frac12\rho q^2\); cylinder streamline hai to \(d\psi=0\) ⇒ \((dw/dz)^2dz\) ka form ban jaata hai.
✍️ Use: Kutta–Joukowski liftUniform stream U past cylinder with circulation Γ: Blasius + residue theorem ⇒ drag X = 0, lift \(=\rho U\Gamma\) (per unit length).

2.5 Circulation aur Kelvin's circulation theorem

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — proof 10 marks
💡 Seedhi baatEk band raaste (closed curve) ke chaaron taraf paani kitna "ghoom" raha hai = circulation. Kelvin kehte hain: ideal liquid me, paani ke saath chalne wale raaste ka circulation kabhi nahi badalta.
📝 Definition + theorem + proof Circulation \(\Gamma=\oint_C\mathbf q\cdot d\mathbf r=\oint(u\,dx+v\,dy+w\,dz)\). Stokes se \(\Gamma=\int_S\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\mathbf n\,dS\).
Kelvin: In an inviscid fluid with conservative body force (\(\mathbf F=-\nabla\Omega\)) and \(\rho\) a function of p only, the circulation round any closed curve moving with the fluid is constant: \(\dfrac{D\Gamma}{Dt}=0\).
  1. \(\frac{D}{Dt}\oint\mathbf q\cdot d\mathbf r=\oint\frac{D\mathbf q}{Dt}\cdot d\mathbf r+\oint\mathbf q\cdot\frac{D}{Dt}(d\mathbf r)\)
  2. \(\frac{D}{Dt}(d\mathbf r)=d\mathbf q\), to doosra term \(=\oint\mathbf q\cdot d\mathbf q=\oint d(\tfrac12q^2)=0\) (closed curve).
  3. Euler se \(\frac{D\mathbf q}{Dt}=-\nabla(\Omega+\int\frac{dp}\rho)\) ⇒ pehla term \(=-\oint d(\Omega+\int\frac{dp}{\rho})=0\).
  4. Isliye \(\frac{D\Gamma}{Dt}=0\). ∎
📝 Permanence of irrotational motionKelvin ka nateeja: agar motion ek baar irrotational hai (har curve par Γ = 0), to hamesha irrotational rahega. Isliye rest se shuru hone wala ideal liquid motion irrotational hota hai.

2.6 Kinetic energy of liquid

⭐ Medium chance
📝 Formula $$T=\frac\rho2\int_V q^2\,dV=\frac\rho2\int_S\phi\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial n}\,dS$$ (n = normal, liquid se bahar ki taraf, yaani body ke andar.) Green's theorem aur \(\nabla^2\phi=0\) se.
Infinite liquid me bahar wali surface (infinity) ka contribution zero hota hai — sirf body ki surface par integral bachta hai.
🧠 Yaad karoKelvin's minimum energy theorem: diye gaye boundary motion ke liye irrotational motion ki KE sabse kam hoti hai.

2.7 Euler's momentum theorem aur D'Alembert paradox

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — D'Alembert paradox
💡 Momentum theoremEk fixed surface ke andar paani ka momentum kitna badha = us par lagne wala force (body force + pressure) − surface se bahar jaata momentum. Steady me: force = momentum ka bahar jaata flux.
💡 D'Alembert paradoxIdeal liquid me koi body steady, uniform chal rahi ho (circulation nahi), to us par koi drag nahi lagta — jabki asal duniya me lagta hai! Yahi "paradox" hai. Wajah: humne viscosity (friction) chhod di hai.
✍️ Cylinder se dikhao
  1. Stream U past cylinder: surface par speed \(q=2U\sin\theta\).
  2. Bernoulli: \(p=p_\infty+\frac12\rho U^2(1-4\sin^2\theta)\).
  3. θ aur π − θ par pressure barabar ⇒ aage aur peeche ka dhakka cancel.
  4. Drag \(=-\int_0^{2\pi}p\cos\theta\,a\,d\theta=0\). ∎

Unit III — Motion of Cylinders

Circular aur elliptic cylinder paani me chale ya paani unke paas se guzre.

3.1 Milne-Thomson circle theorem

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — sabse important theorem
💡 Seedhi baatEk flow f(z) diya hai. Usme radius a ka cylinder rakh do — naya flow kya hoga? Bas ek formula!
📝 Statement + proof Let f(z) be the complex potential of a flow with no singularities in \(|z|\le a\). If a circular cylinder \(|z|=a\) is introduced, the complex potential becomes $$w=f(z)+\bar f\!\left(\frac{a^2}{z}\right).$$
  1. Circle par \(z\bar z=a^2\) ⇒ \(\frac{a^2}{z}=\bar z\).
  2. To circle par \(w=f(z)+\bar f(\bar z)=f(z)+\overline{f(z)}\) = real.
  3. Imaginary part \(\psi=0\) ⇒ circle ek streamline ⇒ paani paar nahi jaata ✓.
  4. \(|z|\gt a\) me \(\bar f(a^2/z)\) ki singularities nahi aati (woh circle ke andar hain), aur infinity par original flow wapas ✓. ∎
✍️ Example: stream past fixed circular cylinder
  1. \(f(z)=-Uz\) ⇒ \(\bar f(a^2/z)=-U\frac{a^2}{z}\).
  2. \(\boxed{w=-U\left(z+\frac{a^2}{z}\right)}\)
  3. \(\phi=-U\left(r+\frac{a^2}{r}\right)\cos\theta,\ \psi=-U\left(r-\frac{a^2}{r}\right)\sin\theta\)
  4. Surface par speed \(q=2U\sin\theta\); stagnation points θ = 0, π; max speed 2U at θ = π/2.
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"State and prove Milne-Thomson circle theorem. Hence find the flow past a circular cylinder / image of a source in a circle."

3.2 Circular cylinder moving in liquid at rest

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
💡 Seedhi baatCylinder U speed se chal raha hai, paani door se ruka hua. Cylinder ko apne saath kuch paani bhi dhakelna padta hai — isliye woh "bhaari" lagta hai. Is extra mass ko added (virtual) mass kehte hain.
📝 Result $$w=\frac{Ua^2}{z},\qquad \phi=\frac{Ua^2}{r}\cos\theta$$ (Check: \(-\partial\phi/\partial r=U\cos\theta\) at r = a — cylinder ki normal velocity ✓.)
KE of liquid (per unit length): \(T=\frac12\pi\rho a^2U^2=\frac12M'U^2\), \(M'=\pi\rho a^2\) = hataaye gaye paani ka mass.
Equation of motion: \((M+M')\dfrac{dU}{dt}=F\) (external force).
✍️ ExampleCylinder density σ, paani ρ, gravity me upar/neeche: \((\sigma+\rho)\frac{dU}{dt}=(\sigma-\rho)g\) ⇒ acceleration \(\frac{\sigma-\rho}{\sigma+\rho}g\).

3.3 Elliptic cylinder

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baatEllipse ke liye elliptic coordinates lete hain: \(z=c\cosh\zeta\), \(\zeta=\xi+i\eta\). Cylinder ki surface \(\xi=\alpha\), jahan \(a=c\cosh\alpha,\ b=c\sinh\alpha\) (semi-axes), \(c^2=a^2-b^2\).
📝 Results
  • Stream U (major axis ke along) past fixed elliptic cylinder: \(w=-U(a+b)\cosh(\zeta-\alpha)\). (Check: \(\xi=\alpha\) par \(\cosh(i\eta)=\cos\eta\) real ⇒ ψ = 0 ✓.)
  • Elliptic cylinder moving with velocity U along major axis (a) in liquid at rest: KE \(=\frac12\pi\rho b^2U^2\) (added mass \(\pi\rho b^2\)).
  • Minor axis ke along: KE \(=\frac12\pi\rho a^2U^2\).
🧠 TrickAdded mass me doosri axis aati hai — major axis ke along chalo to \(b^2\), minor ke along to \(a^2\). (Circle me a = b ⇒ \(\pi\rho a^2\) ✓.)

3.4 Initial motion between two coaxial cylinders

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baatEk cylinder (radius a) doosre bade cylinder (radius b) ke andar hai, beech me paani. Andar wala achanak U velocity se chale to paani ka motion aur KE?
📝 Method + result
  1. \(\phi=\left(Ar+\frac Br\right)\cos\theta\) lo (Laplace ka solution).
  2. r = a par \(-\partial\phi/\partial r=U\cos\theta\); r = b par \(-\partial\phi/\partial r=0\) (outer fixed).
  3. Hal karo: \(A=\frac{Ua^2}{b^2-a^2},\ B=\frac{Ua^2b^2}{b^2-a^2}\).
  4. KE \(=\frac12\pi\rho a^2U^2\,\dfrac{b^2+a^2}{b^2-a^2}\).
Check: \(b\to\infty\) ⇒ \(\frac12\pi\rho a^2U^2\) (akela cylinder) ✓.

Unit IV — 3-D Motion: Spheres & Stokes's Stream Function

Gola (sphere) paani me chale ya paani gole ke paas se guzre.

4.1 Irrotational motion in 3-D (axisymmetric)

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baatSphere wale problems me flow ek axis ke around symmetric hota hai, to spherical coordinates (r, θ) lete hain. \(\nabla^2\phi=0\) ke solutions: $$\phi=\left(Ar^n+\frac{B}{r^{n+1}}\right)P_n(\cos\theta)$$ Sphere problems me zyadatar n = 1 (\(P_1=\cos\theta\)): \(\phi=\left(Ar+\frac{B}{r^2}\right)\cos\theta\).

4.2 Sphere moving in liquid at rest

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
📝 Derivation steps
  1. Sphere radius a, velocity U (θ = 0 disha me). \(\phi=\frac{B}{r^2}\cos\theta\) lo (infinity par 0).
  2. Surface par normal velocity barabar: \(-\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial r}\Big|_{r=a}=U\cos\theta\) ⇒ \(\frac{2B}{a^3}=U\) ⇒ \(B=\frac{Ua^3}{2}\).
  3. \(\boxed{\phi=\dfrac{Ua^3}{2r^2}\cos\theta}\)
  4. KE \(=\frac{\rho}{2}\int\phi\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial n}dS=\frac13\pi\rho a^3U^2=\frac12\cdot\frac{M'}{2}U^2\), jahan \(M'=\frac43\pi a^3\rho\).
Matlab sphere ka added mass = hataaye gaye paani ka aadha.
🧠 TrickCylinder: added mass = poora displaced mass. Sphere: added mass = aadha displaced mass.

4.3 Liquid streaming past a fixed sphere

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
📝 Result Uniform stream U past fixed sphere radius a: $$\phi=-U\left(r+\frac{a^3}{2r^2}\right)\cos\theta$$
  1. Door par \(\phi\approx-Ur\cos\theta=-Ux\) ⇒ velocity U ✓.
  2. r = a par \(\partial\phi/\partial r=-U(1-1)\cos\theta=0\) ✓ (sphere ke paar flow nahi).
  3. Surface par speed \(q=\frac32U\sin\theta\); max \(\frac32U\) at θ = π/2.
  4. Pressure (Bernoulli): \(p=p_\infty+\frac12\rho U^2\left(1-\frac94\sin^2\theta\right)\).
🧠 CompareCylinder ke paas max speed 2U, sphere ke paas 1.5U.

4.4 Equation of motion of sphere — gravity ke under

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — numerical
💡 Seedhi baatGola (density σ) paani (density ρ) me gir raha hai. Neeche kheenchne wala = weight − buoyancy. Par gole ko apne saath aadha displaced paani bhi hilana padta hai.
📝 Equation V = volume. \(\left(\sigma V+\frac12\rho V\right)\dfrac{dU}{dt}=(\sigma-\rho)Vg\) $$\frac{dU}{dt}=\frac{2(\sigma-\rho)}{2\sigma+\rho}\,g$$
✍️ ExampleHawa se bhara halka gola (σ ≈ 0) paani me chhodo: \(\frac{dU}{dt}=\frac{-2\rho}{\rho}g=-2g\) ⇒ 2g se upar jaata hai (shuru me).

4.5 Concentric spheres

⭐ Medium chance
📝 Result Sphere radius a, velocity U, ek fixed concentric sphere radius b ke andar; beech me paani.
  1. \(\phi=\left(Ar+\frac{B}{r^2}\right)\cos\theta\).
  2. r = a par \(-\phi_r=U\cos\theta\); r = b par \(\phi_r=0\).
  3. KE \(=\frac13\pi\rho a^3U^2\,\dfrac{b^3+2a^3}{b^3-a^3}\).
Check: \(b\to\infty\) ⇒ \(\frac13\pi\rho a^3U^2\) (akela sphere) ✓.

4.6 Stokes's stream function

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — definition + sphere example
💡 Seedhi baat3-D me axis ke around symmetric flow ke liye bhi ek stream function hota hai — Stokes's stream function ψ. ψ = constant surfaces hi stream surfaces. Do surfaces ke beech flow = \(2\pi(\psi_2-\psi_1)\).
📝 Definition (spherical r, θ) $$q_r=\frac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial\theta},\qquad q_\theta=-\frac{1}{r\sin\theta}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r}$$ (Sign convention book ke hisaab se badal sakta hai.)
FlowStokes ψ
Uniform stream U\(\frac12Ur^2\sin^2\theta\)
3-D source m\(-m\cos\theta\)
Stream past fixed sphere\(\frac12U\sin^2\theta\left(r^2-\frac{a^3}{r}\right)\)
✍️ CheckSphere wale ψ me r = a rakho: \(r^2-\frac{a^3}{r}=a^2-a^2=0\) ⇒ ψ = 0 ⇒ sphere ek stream surface ✓.
