Daily routine

  1. Roz 2–3 topics: pehle 💡 Concept, phir 📖 Definition aur 📜 Theorem, phir ✒️ Proof copy par likh kar.
  2. Har topic ke baad "padh liya ✓" tick kijiye — progress isi phone par save rehti hai.
  3. Exam se 2 din pehle Model Paper 3 ghante me likhiye, phir solution se milaiye.
  4. Exam se ek din pehle sirf Formula Sheet aur saare theorem statements.

Model papers practice ke liye hain (typical UP university pattern); yeh kisi university ke asli pichle saal ke papers nahi hain. Asli paper ki photo milne par use solution ke saath joda ja sakta hai.
