Unit 1 — Functions of Several Variables & Riemann Integral

f(x, y) jaise functions, unka derivative, aur area nikalne ka tareeka.

1.1 Several variables ka function

📌 Kam chance (par aage sab isi par based hai)
💡 Seedhi baat Ab tak \(f(x)\) padha — ek input, ek output. Ab \(f(x,y)\) hai — do input, ek output.
Real life: kamre ka temperature aapki jagah \((x, y)\) par depend karta hai. Temperature \(= f(x,y)\). Bas!
✍️ Example \(f(x,y) = x^2 + y\). To \(f(2,3) = 4 + 3 = 7\).
\(\mathbb{R}^n\) = n numbers ka group. \(f(x,y) = (x+y,\ xy)\) me input 2 numbers, output 2 numbers, yaani \(f:\mathbb{R}^2\to\mathbb{R}^2\).

1.2 Continuity — "jhatka nahi lagna chahiye"

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — example wala sawaal
💡 Seedhi baat Input thoda badlo to output bhi thoda hi badle — graph me koi tootna nahi.
Ek variable me x sirf left-right se aata hai. Do variables me point \((x,y)\) har raaste se \((0,0)\) ke paas aa sakta hai — line se, parabola se. Har raaste par same answer aana chahiye.
✍️ Example (sabse famous) \(f(x,y)=\dfrac{xy}{x^2+y^2}\), aur \(f(0,0)=0\). Kya \((0,0)\) par continuous hai?
  1. Raasta \(y = mx\) lo: \(f = \dfrac{x\cdot mx}{x^2+m^2x^2} = \dfrac{m}{1+m^2}\).
  2. m = 0 (x-axis) par answer 0. m = 1 (line y = x) par answer 1/2.
  3. Alag raaste, alag answer — isliye limit exist nahi karti.
  4. Answer: f, (0,0) par continuous nahi hai.
🧠 TrickDiscontinuity dikhani ho to y = mx rakho. Answer me m bacha to limit nahi hai.
📝 Exam me aise likho Let \(E\subset\mathbb{R}^n\) be open and \(f:E\to\mathbb{R}^m\). f is continuous at \(a\in E\) if for every \(\varepsilon\gt 0\) there exists \(\delta\gt 0\) such that \(\|x-a\|\lt\delta \Rightarrow \|f(x)-f(a)\|\lt\varepsilon\).
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai "Show that \(f(x,y)=\frac{xy}{x^2+y^2}\) is not continuous at (0,0) though its partial derivatives exist." (5 marks)

1.3 Partial derivative — "ek variable ko freeze karo"

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baat x ke respect me derivative lena hai? y ko constant (number) maan lo aur normal derivative le lo. Bas itna hi.
✍️ Example \(f(x,y) = x^2y + 3y^2\)
  • \(\partial f/\partial x\): y constant. \(x^2y \to 2xy\), \(3y^2 \to 0\). Answer: 2xy
  • \(\partial f/\partial y\): x constant. \(x^2y\to x^2\), \(3y^2\to 6y\). Answer: \(x^2+6y\)
📝 Formula $$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(a,b)=\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{f(a+h,b)-f(a,b)}{h}$$
⚠️ Dhyan do Upar wale \(\frac{xy}{x^2+y^2}\) ke (0,0) par dono partial derivatives exist karte hain (dono 0), phir bhi function continuous nahi. Matlab partial derivative hona kaafi nahi — isliye "differentiability" chahiye.

1.4 Differentiability — derivative ek "matrix" hai

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
💡 Seedhi baat Ek curve ko bahut zoom karo to woh seedhi line jaisa dikhta hai (tangent). Surface ko zoom karo to flat plane jaisa dikhna chahiye. Dikhe to function differentiable.
Us flat approximation ko ek matrix se likhte hain — Jacobian matrix. Isliye kehte hain "derivative ek linear transformation hai".
✍️ Jacobian nikalo \(f(x,y) = (x^2y,\ x+y)\). Har output ka har input se partial derivative:
∂/∂x∂/∂y
\(f_1 = x^2y\)2xy\(x^2\)

Yahi 2×2 table Jacobian matrix \(f'(x,y)\) hai.
📝 Definition \(f:E\to\mathbb{R}^m\) (E open in \(\mathbb{R}^n\)) is differentiable at x if there exists a linear transformation \(A:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m\) such that $$\lim_{h\to 0}\frac{\|f(x+h)-f(x)-Ah\|}{\|h\|}=0.$$ Then \(A=f'(x)\) is the derivative of f at x.
🧠 Seedhi (ladder) yaad karo
  1. Partial derivatives exist + continuous ⇒ Differentiable
  2. Differentiable ⇒ Continuous, aur partial derivatives exist
  3. Ulta hamesha sach nahi.

Theorem: Derivative unique hota hai (proof)

📝 Proof steps
  1. Maan lo do derivatives \(A_1, A_2\) hain. \(B = A_1 - A_2\).
  2. Triangle inequality se \(\dfrac{\|Bh\|}{\|h\|}\to 0\) as \(h\to 0\).
  3. \(h = tu\) lo (u fixed, \(t\to0\)). B linear hai to \(\dfrac{\|Bh\|}{\|h\|} = \dfrac{\|Bu\|}{\|u\|}\) — yeh t par depend hi nahi karta.
  4. Jo t par depend na kare aur 0 ki taraf jaaye, woh 0 hai. To \(Bu=0\) har u ke liye.
  5. Isliye \(B=0\), yaani \(A_1=A_2\). ∎
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai "Define derivative of \(f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m\). Prove that it is unique." / "Find the Jacobian matrix of …"

1.5 Chain rule

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baat z, x aur y par depend; x aur y, t par depend. To z ka t se badlav = (z ka x se × x ka t se) + (z ka y se × y ka t se). Chain ki tarah multiply. $$\frac{dz}{dt}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt}$$
✍️ Example \(z=x^2+y^2,\ x=\cos t,\ y=\sin t\)
  1. \(z_x = 2x,\ z_y = 2y\)
  2. \(dx/dt = -\sin t,\ dy/dt = \cos t\)
  3. \(dz/dt = 2\cos t(-\sin t) + 2\sin t\cos t = 0\)
  4. Check: \(z = \cos^2t+\sin^2t = 1\), constant. Sahi!
📝 Statement If f is differentiable at \(x_0\) and g is differentiable at \(f(x_0)\), then \(F=g\circ f\) is differentiable at \(x_0\) and \(F'(x_0)=g'(f(x_0))\,f'(x_0)\).

1.6 Taylor's theorem (do variables)

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — expansion ka numerical
💡 Seedhi baat Ek variable me: \(f(a+h) = f(a) + hf'(a) + \frac{h^2}{2!}f''(a)+\dots\)
Do variables me bilkul same — bas derivative ki jagah \(\left(h\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+k\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)\) aata hai. Second degree tak khol kar: $$f(a+h,b+k)\approx f+hf_x+kf_y+\frac{1}{2!}\left(h^2f_{xx}+2hk\,f_{xy}+k^2f_{yy}\right)$$ (sab derivatives point (a, b) par)
✍️ Example: \(e^x\sin y\) ko (0,0) ke paas second degree tak

Yeh sirf t ka function hai, to one-variable Maclaurin lagao. Chain rule se \(\varphi'(t)=(h\partial_x+k\partial_y)f\). Phir t = 1 rakho. ∎
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai "Expand \(e^x \sin y\) / \(e^x\cos y\) / \(x^2y+3y-2\) in powers of x and y up to second degree."

1.7 Riemann integral — "rectangles se area"

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
💡 Seedhi baat Curve ke neeche ka area chahiye. Interval [a, b] ko tukdon me kaato — isko partition P kehte hain. Har tukde par rectangle:
  • Upper sum U: har tukde ki sabse oonchi value (\(M_i\)) se — area se zyada aata hai.
  • Lower sum L: har tukde ki sabse neechi value (\(m_i\)) se — area se kam aata hai.
$$U(P,f)=\sum M_i\,\Delta x_i,\qquad L(P,f)=\sum m_i\,\Delta x_i$$ Zyada tukde karo to U neeche aata hai, L upar jaata hai. Dono ek number par mil jaayein to function integrable, aur wahi number integral.
✍️ Example: \(f(x)=x\) on [0, 1]
  1. n barabar tukde, har tukda 1/n.
  2. Upper: \(U=\frac1n(\frac1n+\frac2n+\dots+\frac nn)=\frac{n+1}{2n}\)
  3. Lower: \(L=\frac1n(0+\frac1n+\dots+\frac{n-1}n)=\frac{n-1}{2n}\)
  4. \(n\to\infty\): dono \(\frac12\). \(\int_0^1x\,dx=\frac12\) (triangle ka area bhi 1/2!)
📝 Riemann criterion (bahut important) A bounded function f is Riemann integrable on [a, b] if and only if for every \(\varepsilon\gt0\) there exists a partition P such that \(U(P,f)-L(P,f)\lt\varepsilon\).
✍️ Non-integrable example (zaroor yaad karo) Dirichlet function: f(x) = 1 agar x rational, 0 agar irrational, [0,1] par.
  1. Har chhote tukde me rational bhi hain, irrational bhi.
  2. To har tukde me \(M_i=1,\ m_i=0\).
  3. U = 1, L = 0 — kitne bhi tukde karo.
  4. Upper ≠ lower, isliye integrable nahi.
🧠 Kaun integrable haiContinuous ✔ · Monotonic ✔ · Finite jumps wala bounded ✔ · Dirichlet ✘
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai "State and prove the necessary and sufficient condition for Riemann integrability." / "Show that Dirichlet's function is not R-integrable." / "Every continuous function is integrable — prove."

1.8 Properties of Riemann integral

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baatSab wahi jo normal integration me hota hai:
  • \(\int(f+g)=\int f+\int g\), \(\int cf = c\int f\)
  • \(f\le g\Rightarrow \int f\le\int g\)
  • \(\int_a^b=\int_a^c+\int_c^b\)
  • \(|\int f|\le\int|f|\)
  • \(|f|\le M \Rightarrow |\int_a^b f|\le M(b-a)\)
  • f, g integrable ⇒ fg integrable

1.9 Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — proof aata hai
💡 Seedhi baat Integration aur differentiation ulte kaam hain.
First FTC: \(F(x)=\int_a^x f(t)dt\) = a se x tak area. x thoda badhao to area f(x) ki speed se badhta hai, isliye \(F'(x)=f(x)\).
Second FTC: Agar \(F'=f\) to \(\int_a^bf=F(b)-F(a)\). Yahi roz use karte hain: \(\int_0^2x^2dx=\frac83\).
📝 Second FTC ka proof Statement: If f is Riemann integrable on [a, b] and F is differentiable with F′ = f, then \(\int_a^b f\,dx = F(b)-F(a)\).
  1. \(\varepsilon\gt0\) lo. Riemann criterion se P lo jisme \(U-L\lt\varepsilon\).
  2. Har tukde par Mean Value Theorem: \(F(x_i)-F(x_{i-1})=f(t_i)\Delta x_i\).
  3. Sab jodo: \(\sum f(t_i)\Delta x_i = F(b)-F(a)\) (beech ke terms cancel).
  4. \(m_i\le f(t_i)\le M_i\), isliye \(L\le F(b)-F(a)\le U\).
  5. \(\int f\) bhi L aur U ke beech hai.
  6. Dono chhote gap (\(\lt\varepsilon\)) me, to \(|F(b)-F(a)-\int f|\lt\varepsilon\). ε arbitrary ⇒ barabar. ∎
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"State and prove the fundamental theorem of integral calculus." (10 marks)

Unit 2 — Riemann-Stieltjes (R-S) Integral

Riemann jaisa hi, bas width \(\Delta x\) ki jagah \(\Delta\alpha\).

2.1 R-S integral kya hai? (dukaan wala example)

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — definition
💡 Seedhi baat Dukaan socho. f(x) = daam (price) time x par. α(x) = time x tak kitna maal bika.
  • \(\int f\,dx\) = daam × time — koi matlab nahi.
  • \(\int f\,d\alpha\) = daam × kitna bika = total kamai!
Formula Riemann jaisa, bas \(\Delta\alpha_i=\alpha(x_i)-\alpha(x_{i-1})\): $$U(P,f,\alpha)=\sum M_i\,\Delta\alpha_i,\qquad L(P,f,\alpha)=\sum m_i\,\Delta\alpha_i$$
📝 Definition Let α be monotonically increasing on [a, b] and f bounded. If \(\inf_P U(P,f,\alpha)=\sup_P L(P,f,\alpha)\), then f is Riemann–Stieltjes integrable w.r.t. α, written \(f\in R(\alpha)\), and the common value is \(\int_a^b f\,d\alpha\).
🧠 Yaad rakhoα hamesha increasing (taaki \(\Delta\alpha\ge0\)). Agar α(x) = x, to yeh normal Riemann integral hi hai.

2.2 Jab α smooth ho — sabse aasaan calculation

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — numerical
💡 Seedhi baat\(d\alpha = \alpha'(x)\,dx\). Bas badal do aur normal integration karo! $$\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=\int_a^b f(x)\,\alpha'(x)\,dx$$
✍️ Example 1: \(\int_0^1 x\,d(x^2)\)
  1. \(\alpha=x^2\Rightarrow\alpha'=2x\)
  2. \(\int_0^1x\cdot2x\,dx=\int_0^12x^2dx\)
  3. \(=\left[\frac{2x^3}{3}\right]_0^1=\boxed{\frac23}\)
✍️ Example 2: \(\int_0^{\pi/2} x\,d(\sin x)\)
  1. \(\alpha'=\cos x\)
  2. \(\int x\cos x\,dx = x\sin x+\cos x\) (by parts)
  3. \(\left(\frac\pi2+0\right)-(0+1)=\boxed{\frac\pi2-1}\)

2.3 Jab α jump kare — greatest integer [x]

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — numerical
💡 Seedhi baat [x] (greatest integer) x = 1, 2, 3… par 1 ka jump karta hai, baaki jagah flat. Flat jagah \(\Delta\alpha = 0\), to kuch nahi milta. Sirf jump wali jagah se kamai.
Rule: \(\int f\,d\alpha = \sum (\text{jump ka size})\times f(\text{jump point})\)
✍️ Example 1: \(\int_0^3 x\,d[x]\)
  1. 0 ke baad 3 tak jump: x = 1, 2, 3 par, har jump = 1.
  2. Answer \(=f(1)+f(2)+f(3)=1+2+3=\boxed{6}\)
✍️ Example 2\(\int_0^3 x^2\,d[x]=1+4+9=\boxed{14}\)  ·  \(\int_0^2 e^x\,d[x]=e+e^2\)
📝 Unit step function \(I(x)=0\ (x\le0),\ 1\ (x\gt0)\). If \(a\lt s\lt b\), f bounded and continuous at s, and \(\alpha(x)=I(x-s)\), then \(\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=f(s)\).
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"Evaluate \(\int_0^3 x\,d[x]\)" / "\(\int_0^2 x^2\,d[x]\)" / "\(\int_0^1 x\,d(x^2)\)" — 2 se 5 marks, pakka number.

2.4 Kab integrate kar sakte hain? (Existence)

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — proof
📝 Theorem (Riemann's condition) \(f\in R(\alpha)\) on [a, b] if and only if for every \(\varepsilon\gt0\) there exists a partition P such that \(U(P,f,\alpha)-L(P,f,\alpha)\lt\varepsilon\).
  1. (⇐) \(L\le\) lower integral \(\le\) upper integral \(\le U\). To (upper − lower) \(\le U-L\lt\varepsilon\). ε kitna bhi chhota ⇒ dono barabar ⇒ integrable.
  2. (⇒) Integral = I. \(P_1\) lo jisme \(L(P_1)\gt I-\frac\varepsilon2\), \(P_2\) lo jisme \(U(P_2)\lt I+\frac\varepsilon2\). \(P=P_1\cup P_2\). Zyada tukde se U ghatta, L badhta hai, to \(U(P)-L(P)\lt\varepsilon\). ∎
ConditionResult
f continuous\(f\in R(\alpha)\) — hamesha
f monotonic, α continuous\(f\in R(\alpha)\)
f me finite jumps, un points par α continuous\(f\in R(\alpha)\)
📝 Proof: f continuous ⇒ f ∈ R(α)
  1. Closed interval par continuous ⇒ uniformly continuous.
  2. η > 0 aisa lo ki \(\eta[\alpha(b)-\alpha(a)]\lt\varepsilon\).
  3. Partition itna barik ki har tukde me \(M_i-m_i\le\eta\).
  4. \(U-L=\sum(M_i-m_i)\Delta\alpha_i\le\eta\sum\Delta\alpha_i=\eta[\alpha(b)-\alpha(a)]\lt\varepsilon\). ∎

2.5 Properties of R-S integral

⭐ Medium chance
💡 List (Riemann jaisi)
  • \(\int(f_1+f_2)d\alpha=\int f_1d\alpha+\int f_2d\alpha\); \(\int cf\,d\alpha=c\int f\,d\alpha\)
  • \(f_1\le f_2\Rightarrow\int f_1d\alpha\le\int f_2d\alpha\)
  • \(\int_a^b=\int_a^c+\int_c^b\)
  • \(\int f\,d(\alpha_1+\alpha_2)=\int f\,d\alpha_1+\int f\,d\alpha_2\)
  • \(\int f\,d(c\alpha)=c\int f\,d\alpha\) (c > 0)
  • \(|f|\le M\Rightarrow|\int f\,d\alpha|\le M[\alpha(b)-\alpha(a)]\)
  • By parts: \(\int_a^b f\,dg=f(b)g(b)-f(a)g(a)-\int_a^b g\,df\)

2.6 R-S integral as a limit of sums

⭐ Medium chance
💡 Seedhi baat M ya m lene ki zaroorat nahi. Har tukde me koi bhi point \(t_i\) lo: $$S(P,f,\alpha)=\sum f(t_i)\,\Delta\alpha_i \;\longrightarrow\; \int_a^b f\,d\alpha \quad\text{jab }\mu(P)=\max\Delta x_i\to0$$ Kyun? \(f(t_i)\) hamesha \(m_i\) aur \(M_i\) ke beech, to S bhi L aur U ke beech "sandwich" ho jaata hai.

2.7 Mean Value Theorems

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — First MVT proof
💡 Seedhi baatKul kamai = (koi ek "average daam") × (kul bikri).
📝 First MVT + proof If f is continuous on [a, b] and α increasing, there exists \(\xi\in[a,b]\) with $$\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=f(\xi)\,[\alpha(b)-\alpha(a)].$$
  1. m = min f, M = max f ⇒ \(m\le f\le M\).
  2. Integrate: \(m[\alpha(b)-\alpha(a)]\le\int f\,d\alpha\le M[\alpha(b)-\alpha(a)]\).
  3. Divide karo — beech ka number milta hai. Intermediate Value Theorem se f usko kisi \(\xi\) par leta hai. ∎
📝 Second MVT If α continuous and f monotonic on [a, b], there exists ξ with $$\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=f(a)[\alpha(\xi)-\alpha(a)]+f(b)[\alpha(b)-\alpha(\xi)].$$
🧠 TrickSecond MVT: "a se ξ tak f(a), ξ se b tak f(b)".

2.8 Integration aur differentiation

📌 Kam chance
💡 Seedhi baat\(F(x)=\int_a^x f\,d\alpha\). Jahan α continuous, wahan F continuous. Jahan f continuous aur α differentiable: \(F'(x)=f(x)\alpha'(x)\).

Unit 3 — Countable Sets, Cardinal Numbers & Series

Infinity ko ginna, aur infinite numbers jodna.

3.1 Countable set — "jisko 1, 2, 3… se gin sako"

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — definition
💡 Seedhi baatHar element par 1, 2, 3, 4… ka token laga sako aur koi chhoote nahi — to countable.
✍️ Example
N1234…
Even2468…
Rule n → 2n. Even numbers "aadhe" lagte hain, phir bhi utne hi jitne N!
Integers: 0, 1, −1, 2, −2, 3, −3, … — line me lag gaye, to countable.
📝 Definitions
  • A ~ B (equivalent) if there is a one-one onto map A → B.
  • A is countable if A ~ N.
  • At most countable: finite or countable.
  • Uncountable: neither finite nor countable.

3.2 Q countable hai — proof

🔥 Bahut zyada chance
📝 Proof (height method)
  1. Har positive rational p/q (lowest form) ki height = p + q.
  2. Height 2: 1/1 · Height 3: 1/2, 2/1 · Height 4: 1/3, 3/1 · Height 5: 1/4, 2/3, 3/2, 4/1 …
  3. Har height par finite rationals.
  4. Height-wise likhte jao: 1, 1/2, 2, 1/3, 3, 1/4, 2/3, 3/2, 4, …
  5. Har rational kabhi na kabhi aayega ⇒ positive rationals countable.
  6. 0 aur negatives Z ki tarah beech me daalo ⇒ Q countable. ∎

3.3 R uncountable hai — Cantor ka diagonal trick

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — sabse famous proof
💡 Seedhi baatReal numbers ki kitni bhi lambi list banao, koi na koi hamesha chhoot jaayega.
📝 Proof ((0,1) ke liye kaafi)
  1. Maan lo (0,1) ki poori list ban gayi: \(x_1, x_2, x_3,\dots\)
  2. \(x_1=0.\mathbf{5}137\ldots,\ x_2=0.2\mathbf{8}41\ldots,\ x_3=0.90\mathbf{3}6\ldots,\ x_4=0.442\mathbf{5}\ldots\)
  3. Diagonal digits dekho: 5, 8, 3, 5, …
  4. Naya number y: har diagonal digit badlo — digit 5 nahi to 5 likho, 5 hai to 4. y = 0.4554…
  5. y har \(x_n\) se n-th digit par alag ⇒ list me nahi.
  6. Par y ∈ (0,1) — contradiction. (0,1), aur R, uncountable. ∎
🧠 Yaad karoCountable: N, Z, Q. Uncountable: R, irrationals, [0,1]. Countable sets ka countable union bhi countable.

3.5 Schroeder-Bernstein theorem

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — statement + use
💡 Seedhi baatA ko B me fit kar sako (one-one) aur B ko A me fit kar sako ⇒ dono barabar size. Jaise a ≤ b aur b ≤ a ⇒ a = b.
📝 StatementIf A is equivalent to a subset of B and B is equivalent to a subset of A, then A ~ B.
✍️ Use: (0,1) ~ [0,1]
  1. (0,1) ⊂ [0,1] — seedha fit.
  2. [0,1] → (0,1): \(x\mapsto\frac{x+1}{3}\), [0,1] ko [1/3, 2/3] me bhejta hai, one-one.
  3. Dono taraf fit ⇒ (0,1) ~ [0,1].
💡 Proof ideaf : A→B, g : B→A one-one. Har element ke "ancestors" peeche track karo: jo A me shuru hue un par f, jo B me shuru hue un par \(g^{-1}\). Bijection ban jaata hai.

3.8 Abel's aur Dirichlet's tests

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — proof + example
💡 Seedhi baatJab series \(a_n\times b_n\) jaisi ho (do sequences ka guna):
Test\(\sum a_n\) se chahiye\(b_n\) se chahiye
DirichletPartial sums boundedMonotonic, \(b_n\to0\)
Abel\(\sum a_n\) convergentMonotonic, bounded
🧠 TrickDirichlet me \(a_n\) kamzor (sirf bounded) ⇒ \(b_n\) mazboot (0 tak). Abel me \(a_n\) mazboot (convergent) ⇒ \(b_n\) kamzor chalega (sirf bounded).
✍️ Example: \(\sum\frac{\sin n\theta}{n}\) (θ ≠ 2kπ)
  1. \(a_n=\sin n\theta,\ b_n=\frac1n\).
  2. \(|\sin\theta+\dots+\sin n\theta|\le\frac{1}{|\sin(\theta/2)|}\) — bounded.
  3. 1/n ghatta aur 0 tak jaata hai.
  4. Dirichlet se converge.
📝 Abel's lemma + Dirichlet proof \(A_n=a_1+\dots+a_n\): $$\sum_{k=1}^n a_kb_k=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}A_k(b_k-b_{k+1})+A_nb_n$$ \(|A_k|\le M\). Tail ko is lemma se likho; \(b_k-b_{k+1}\ge0\) telescope hokar \(\le 2M\,b_{n+1}\) deta hai. \(b_n\to0\) ⇒ tail \(\lt\varepsilon\) ⇒ Cauchy criterion se converge. ∎

📝 Results

3.10 Rearrangement — order badalna

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — Riemann's theorem
💡 Seedhi baat2 + 3 = 3 + 2. Par infinite series me order badalne se answer badal sakta hai!
  • Absolutely convergent: kaise bhi order — sum same (Dirichlet).
  • Conditionally convergent: order badal kar koi bhi number, ya ∞ (Riemann).
✍️ Example 1 − 1/2 + 1/3 − 1/4 + … = log 2
Order: do positive, ek negative: (1 + 1/3 − 1/2) + (1/5 + 1/7 − 1/4) + … = (3/2) log 2. Same terms, alag answer!
📝 Riemann theorem proof idea (target S)
  1. Positive terms ka jod ∞, negative terms ka −∞ (warna absolute ho jaati).
  2. Positive jodo jab tak S se upar.
  3. Negative jodo jab tak S se neeche.
  4. Repeat. Terms → 0, to S se doori ghatti jaati ⇒ S par converge. ∎

Unit 4 — Pointwise & Uniform Convergence

Functions ki sequence: \(f_1(x), f_2(x), f_3(x),\dots\) kisi f(x) ki taraf "ek saath" jaati hai ya nahi.

4.1 Pointwise vs Uniform — class wala example

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — definitions + difference
💡 Seedhi baat Teacher boli "sab 90% laao".
Pointwise: har student pahunchta hai — koi 2 mahine me, koi 2 saal me. Sab pahunche, par ek fixed time nahi.
Uniform: "10 mahine baad poori class ek saath 90% par" — sabke liye ek fixed time (ek hi N).
🧠 PictureLimit f ke graph ke upar-neeche ε ki patti banao. Uniform = kuch n ke baad \(f_n\) ka poora graph patti ke andar.
📝 Definitions Pointwise: for every x ∈ E and ε > 0 there is N(ε, x) with \(|f_n(x)-f(x)|\lt\varepsilon\) for n ≥ N.
Uniform: for every ε > 0 there is N(ε), independent of x, with \(|f_n(x)-f(x)|\lt\varepsilon\) for all n ≥ N and all x ∈ E.
Uniform ⇒ pointwise; converse false.

4.2 \(M_n\)-test — uniform check karne ka sabse aasaan tareeka

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — numericals pakke
💡 Seedhi baat\(f_n\) aur f ke beech sabse bada gap nikalo. Woh bhi 0 ki taraf jaaye ⇒ uniform. $$M_n=\sup_{x\in E}|f_n(x)-f(x)|$$$$f_n\to f\text{ uniformly}\iff M_n\to0$$
🧠 4 steps — har sawaal me
  1. Limit f(x) nikalo (n → ∞).
  2. Gap \(g(x)=|f_n-f|\).
  3. g ka maximum (derivative = 0) = \(M_n\).
  4. \(M_n\to0\)? Haan ⇒ uniform, nahi ⇒ not uniform.
✍️ Example 1 (uniform): \(f_n=\frac{x}{1+nx^2}\) on R
  1. f(x) = 0.
  2. Gap = \(\frac{|x|}{1+nx^2}\).
  3. Max at \(x=\frac1{\sqrt n}\): value \(\frac{1}{2\sqrt n}\).
  4. \(M_n=\frac1{2\sqrt n}\to0\) ⇒ uniform.
✍️ Example 2 (NOT uniform — sabse famous): \(f_n=x^n\) on [0,1]
  1. 0 ≤ x < 1: \(x^n\to0\). x = 1: 1. To f = 0 (x < 1), f(1) = 1.
  2. Gap = \(x^n\) (x < 1).
  3. x = 0.9999 lo — \(x^n\) lagbhag 1. To \(M_n=1\).
  4. \(M_n\not\to0\) ⇒ not uniform.
Lesson: har \(x^n\) continuous, par limit me x = 1 par jump. Uniform hota to aisa nahi hota.
✍️ Example 3: \(f_n=\frac{nx}{1+n^2x^2}\) on [0,1] f = 0. x = 1/n par \(f_n=\frac12\) ⇒ \(M_n\ge\frac12\) ⇒ [0,1] par not uniform. [a,1] (a > 0) par uniform.
❓ Sawaal aise aata hai"Show that \(\{x^n\}\) is not uniformly convergent on [0,1] but uniformly on [0, k], k < 1." / "Test uniform convergence of \(\frac{x}{1+nx^2}\)."

4.3 Cauchy criterion for uniform convergence

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — proof
💡 Seedhi baatLimit f pata na ho tab bhi: aage ke \(f_n\) aur \(f_m\) aapas me (sab x par ek saath) bahut paas hon.
📝 Statement + proof \(\{f_n\}\) converges uniformly on E iff for every ε > 0 there is N with \(|f_n(x)-f_m(x)|\lt\varepsilon\) for all m, n ≥ N, all x ∈ E.
  1. (⇒) \(|f_n-f|\lt\frac\varepsilon2\) sab x par. \(|f_n-f_m|\le|f_n-f|+|f-f_m|\lt\varepsilon\).
  2. (⇐) Har x par \(\{f_n(x)\}\) Cauchy ⇒ converge, f(x) kaho. Condition me m → ∞: \(|f_n(x)-f(x)|\le\varepsilon\) sab x, sab n ≥ N ⇒ uniform. ∎

4.4 Weierstrass M-test — series ka hero

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — har paper me
💡 Seedhi baatx hata do — har term ka sabse bada possible size \(M_n\) nikalo. \(\sum M_n\) converge ⇒ function series uniformly converge.
📝 Statement + proof If \(|u_n(x)|\le M_n\) for all x ∈ E, all n, and \(\sum M_n\) converges, then \(\sum u_n(x)\) converges uniformly and absolutely on E.
Proof: \(\sum M_n\) Cauchy: \(M_{n+1}+\dots+M_m\lt\varepsilon\). Har x par \(|u_{n+1}(x)+\dots+u_m(x)|\le M_{n+1}+\dots+M_m\lt\varepsilon\). N, x par depend nahi ⇒ uniform. ∎
✍️ Examples
  • \(\sum\frac{\sin nx}{n^2}\): \(|\cdot|\le\frac1{n^2}\), \(\sum\frac1{n^2}\) conv ⇒ uniform on R.
  • \(\sum\frac{\cos nx}{n^p}\), p > 1 ⇒ uniform on R.
  • \(\sum\frac{1}{n^2+x^2}\le\frac1{n^2}\) ⇒ uniform.
  • \(\sum x^n\) on [−r, r], r < 1: \(|x^n|\le r^n\) ⇒ uniform.

4.6 Uniform convergence ke 3 fayde

🔥 Bahut zyada chance — continuity wala proof
📝 Fayda 1 — Continuity (ε/3 proof) Each \(f_n\) continuous and \(f_n\to f\) uniformly ⇒ f continuous.
  1. N aisa ki sab x par \(|f-f_N|\lt\frac\varepsilon3\).
  2. \(f_N\) continuous: \(|x-x_0|\lt\delta\Rightarrow|f_N(x)-f_N(x_0)|\lt\frac\varepsilon3\).
  3. Teen chhalang:
$$|f(x)-f(x_0)|\le|f(x)-f_N(x)|+|f_N(x)-f_N(x_0)|+|f_N(x_0)-f(x_0)|\lt\varepsilon\quad\blacksquare$$
📝 Fayda 2 — Integration $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^b f_n\,dx=\int_a^b f\,dx$$ Proof: \(|\int f_n-\int f|\le\int|f_n-f|\le M_n(b-a)\to0\). Series ko term-by-term integrate kar sakte ho.
⚠️ Fayda 3 — Differentiation (dhyan se) Sirf \(f_n\) uniform kaafi nahi — \(f_n'\) uniform chahiye (aur kisi ek point par \(f_n(x_0)\) converge). Tab \(f'=\lim f_n'\).
Counter-example: \(f_n=\frac{\sin nx}{\sqrt n}\to0\) uniformly, par \(f_n'(0)=\sqrt n\to\infty\).
